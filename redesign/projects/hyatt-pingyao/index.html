01The project

Integrating Pingyao’s local culture experience into modern and fashionable luxury hotel life.

Inspired by the ancient Pingyao culture and the space forms of the courtyards in Shaxi, the design of the hotel public area brings the idea of communal living, meanwhile, the hotel guest room area conveys the notion of ‘on-roof’ living experience. Various sizes of courtyards are located on the roof, linking the hotel function areas and breaking up the guest room into several courtyard-units. Between the courtyards and above the tile houses, the ubiquitous cultural scene and experience of the Jinzhong courtyard provides a modern and traditional cultural feast for hotel consumers.

Qinhuangdao Hotel & Hot Springs

H2 project archive · 2021

InterContinental Hotels Group PLC (IHG)

All work↗
01The project

To create a high-end wild luxury and light healing parent-child hotel, reflecting the design characteristics of fashion, personality and fun.

“One building, two typology”. Combining the characteristics of the hotel and the park.Several small scenes of parent-child lifestyle are planted in the hotel, and indoor entertainment and outdoor leisure venues are combined to create a distinctive parent-child theme hotel. The shape of the building stretches naturally and makes full use of the landscape surface. The facade has both personality and dynamic movement, the volume is dispositioned lively with bright color contrast. Enjoy both relaxing ocean view and forest view, with a large area of outdoor beach and landscaped garden. The featured parent-child scene experience area of the hotel allows guests to not only relax, but also spend a happy time with their families.
